# Defence Hackathon: EUDIS Defence Hackathon 2026, Ireland

This is our team's workspace for **Ireland's first defence hackathon**: the EUDIS Defence Hackathon at DCU:Innovate Hub, Glasnevin, Dublin, **15–17 October 2026**. The theme is *Autonomy on the Battlefield*.

## TL;DR

**Recommended idea: ANCHOR WATCH**, early warning for Ireland's undersea cables and pipelines. It targets **Challenge 3, the Department of Defence's "Space-Enabled Maritime Domain Awareness"**.

ANCHOR WATCH:
- **watches** live ship traffic around Ireland, using AIS data plus satellite radar when ships go dark;
- **spots** the few vessels whose behaviour matches past cable-cutting incidents: dragging anchors, loitering, going dark over infrastructure;
- **predicts** which cable or pipeline segment is at risk, and when;
- **tells** the watch officer which response assets can arrive in time.

It complements what Ireland already funds (Sea-Scan fibre sensing and GUARD ship-launched drones) instead of competing with it.

## Key facts

- **Prizes and support:**
  - €20k prize fund: 1st €10k plus the EUDIS Mentoring Programme; 2nd €6k; 3rd €4k.
  - Follow-on support for several teams: DCU incubation, a route towards ESA BIC funding, test and validation with the Defence Forces RTI unit, TensorX compute credits.
- **Teams:** 3–10 people, on site for all 2.5 days. Every member must be **18+ and a citizen of the EU, Norway or Ukraine** (ID checked on site).
- **Prior work** is welcome, but you must **declare what existed before the event** versus what you built there.
- The Irish jury is "leading Irish defence-tech experts". The Department of Defence wrote Challenge 3.
