# AIS and vessel history

**Where:** ship icon → **AIS ship tracker**; click a ship on the map.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Received ships | Displays available AIS positions, source times and connection status. |
| Vessel card | Shows name, MMSI, speed, course, vessel type, navigation status and available destination information. |
| Search and filters | Find names/MMSIs; filter by freshness, vessel type or speed range. |
| Follow and centre | Centre on a ship, follow received updates or fit the displayed ships. |
| Recorded path | Show the selected ship's retained fixes and fit its past track. Gaps remain separate. |
| Local fleet | Add/remove a ship from your saved local fleet. |
| Vessel photograph | Shows an operator-supplied local image when available; otherwise a placeholder. |
| Feed controls | Connect, disconnect or change the AIS service. Cached positions keep their original times. |
| Team history | **Satellite analysis → Fleet history** lists shared vessel-history checks and review leads, with a review-only filter and page controls. |

Local map history retains up to 24 hours and 720 fixes per vessel, subject to
the overall storage limit. Team database history is separate. Coverage is
partial: a missing report does not mean a vessel is absent, and a recorded
track's beginning does not establish its departure port.
