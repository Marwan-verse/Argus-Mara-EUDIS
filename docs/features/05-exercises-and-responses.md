# Exercises and simulated responses

**Where:** **Exercise** at the bottom of the map, incident review, and
**+ → Response board / Decision review / Timeline and events**.

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Replay controls | Play, pause, restart, change speed or move through the fictional scenario. |
| Event timeline | Jump to key replay events such as an AIS gap, radar observation or alert. |
| Cable countdown | Show expected and earliest approach estimates under stated assumptions. |
| Anchor reach | Focus risk calculations on the assumed anchor-reachable seabed section. |
| Dark prediction | Display a likely area, reachable boundary and assumed position during an AIS gap. Selected stale live contacts can show uncertainty areas too. |
| Response comparison | Compare assumed aircraft, helicopter, ship, drone and other observation options with the remaining time. |
| Send and recall | Record simulated assignments and animate preparing, en-route and on-scene states. |
| Resource conflicts | Show when assigning an asset leaves another fictional incident without timely coverage. |
| Exercise storm | Apply simulated wind restrictions to drones. |
| Human approval | Review the evidence and explicitly approve a simulated option. Export an exercise evidence record. |

Fleet capability, readiness, routes and wind limits are assumptions. Prediction
areas describe possibilities, not known ship positions. These controls never
dispatch real assets. Live weather does not change exercise response timings.
