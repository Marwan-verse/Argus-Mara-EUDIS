# Team conduct

This is a time limited team project. Be respectful in code reviews, issues, and team calls. Focus criticism on the work; explain a concrete change that would help. Share blockers early, give credit for contributions and sources, and make room for teammates to speak.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, and sharing another person's private information are not acceptable. If something happens, contact the team captain or repository owner through a private team channel. They should hear the people involved, keep details limited to those who need them, and take proportionate action, including removing access if necessary.

Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
